What the 1999 Series carries

01

Your number, for life.

The shank is stamped once, with your number and 1999. It is not a sticker and it is not reissued. If the bag comes back for repair in 2044, we will know exactly which one it is.

Numbered 01 – 50

02

First in the queue.

Series commissions are cut before general orders. When a good hide comes in, it goes to the Series bench first — which is the only meaningful kind of priority in a workshop this size.

Cut first, always

03

Stamped, not printed.

Your initials go into brass with a steel punch and a hammer, three characters, once. Printing wears off in a season. This does not, and after ten years the letters hold the polish differently to the rest of the shank.

Three characters, in brass

04

Repaired, always.

Bring it back whenever it needs it, for as long as you own it. Stitching, handles, feet, lining — we made it, so we can open it. You pay for materials on anything that is honest wear.

Lifetime repair, in-house

Inside The Piece

Built Where You Cannot See.

  • Full-grain interior Vegetable-tanned cowhide, 1.2 mm — a lining that outlives most bags' exteriors.
  • Structured pockets Cut and stitched to hold their shape empty, so nothing collapses into the base.
  • 14″ laptop sleeve Padded and leather-faced, set away from the base seam where knocks land.
  • Reinforced base A second leather panel and brass feet, because bags are put down on concrete.

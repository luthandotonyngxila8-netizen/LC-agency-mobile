The house

We make fewer things,
slowly.

LASS began in a two-room laboratory with a stubborn question: how little can a formula contain and still change the skin? Six years later the answer is nine products, each blended in batches of 400 and dated by hand.

No seasonal launches. No ingredient added for the label. When a formula can be improved we reformulate it and tell you exactly what changed — down to the percentage.

Inside the formula

Four ingredients
do most of the work

Everything else in the bottle is there to keep those four stable, tolerable and absorbable. Tap through to see what each one is doing, and at what percentage.

3%
bakuchiol

A plant-derived retinol alternative from Psoralea corylifolia. Dosed at 3% in Renewal Serum No.1 — the concentration used in the twelve-week trial that showed comparable smoothing to 0.5% retinol, without the peeling or the sun sensitivity.

Cold-pressed in Limpopo from fruit gathered by a cooperative of 340 women. Exceptionally high in oleic acid and natural tocopherols, which is why our oils stay stable for 24 months without synthetic antioxidants.

Barrier lipids in the 3:1:1 ratio skin builds them in. Ceramide Veil carries 2% total ceramide complex — enough to measurably reduce water loss within four hours, verified by corneometer testing on 32 volunteers.

Unfermented green rooibos from Clanwilliam, standardised for aspalathin. It calms the visible flush that follows actives, which is what lets us pair a retinoid alternative with acids in the same routine.

The ritual

Four minutes, twice a day

Order matters more than quantity. This is the sequence we formulate against — thin to rich, water to oil.

Dissolve

Marula Cleansing Balm on dry skin, sixty seconds, warm cloth. Removes the day without stripping the barrier.

Prepare

Hydra-Dew Essence pressed into damp skin. Humectants work in water — never on a dry face.

Treat

Four drops of Renewal Serum No.1. Nightly once your skin has been introduced to it over two weeks.

Seal

Ceramide Veil, then Midnight Repair Oil on the driest areas. The oil goes last, always.

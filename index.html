Our story

It started with
one woman's skin

LASS began in 2020 out of a personal fight with acne and dark spots — and the discovery that turmeric, used properly and in the right company, could help brighten and calm skin that had stopped responding to everything else.

That first formula became a range. Today LASS is a registered South African business making natural skincare for acne, hyperpigmentation, scarring and dark marks, sold directly and through a growing network of consultants across the country.

Inside the formulas

Five ingredients
we keep coming back to

Nothing exotic and nothing hidden — kitchen-cupboard botanicals with a long history in skincare, blended so they stay gentle enough to use often.

Turmeric
led

The ingredient LASS was built on. Turmeric is prized in skincare for its brightening and soothing properties, which is why it runs through the scrub, the soap and the mask — the three products people most often start with.

A natural humectant that draws moisture into the skin and takes the edge off turmeric's intensity. It is what makes the face mask comfortable enough for weekly use on sensitive skin.

The active in our Brightening Serum, used to fade the dark marks left behind by breakouts and to even out tone over time. Apply it in the morning and follow with sun protection.

Brightening and mildly astringent, which suits skin that gets oily through the day. It carries our exfoliating face wash, where it lifts dullness without leaving skin tight.

Rich, antioxidant and deeply conditioning — the base of our body butter, for elbows, knees and anywhere the winter has been unkind.

The routine

Four steps, morning and night

You do not need everything at once. Start with the wash and the day cream, then add the serum when your skin has settled.

Wash

Lemon Exfoliating Face Wash, morning and evening, on damp skin.

Polish

Turmeric Facial Scrub two or three times a week — never on broken or freshly shaved skin.

Treat

Brightening Serum on the marks that outlast the pimple. Give it weeks, not days.

Protect

Anti-Blemish Day Cream to finish, then sunscreen. Brightening without sun protection is work undone.

Weekly extra: the Turmeric & Honey Face Mask, fifteen minutes, rinsed with warm water.

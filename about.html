A standard, not a story

Every claim on this site is one we can produce paperwork for. Here is what we hold ourselves to, and what happens when we fall short.

Origin

It started because a cosmetic chemist got tired of being asked to hide the percentages.

Thandi Meyer spent eleven years formulating for houses whose briefs always arrived the same way: make it feel expensive, make the ingredient list look impressive, keep the actives low enough to be cheap and safe from complaints. In 2019 she rented two rooms in Salt River, bought a homogeniser at auction and made 400 units of a single serum with one active, dosed at the level in the trial paper.

It sold out in nine days, mostly to dermatology nurses. That serum is now Renewal No.1, and the rule it was made under has not changed: if we cannot cite the dose, we do not make the claim.

The standard

Five commitments

01

Dose before decoration

Every active is printed at percentage on the carton and the product page. If an ingredient is present below its effective range, it is not listed as a feature — it is listed as what it is: a solvent, a preservative, a texture agent.

02

A named grower for every botanical

Marula from the Nkomazi cooperative, green rooibos from the Bergman farm in Clanwilliam, buchu from two Cederberg smallholders. Harvest year appears on the batch label.

03

Batches of 400, dated by hand

Small enough that our formulator signs off on each one, and that nothing sits in a warehouse long enough to need extra preservative to survive it.

04

Refillable, permanently

We will not discontinue a refill for a vessel we have sold. If a formula is retired, the last refill run stays available for three years.

05

We publish the changes

Reformulations go out to the list before they ship, with the old and new percentages side by side. Twice we have removed an ingredient and said so. We would rather lose the sale than the benefit of the doubt.

Testing

What we test, and on whom

Never on animals — including in markets that would require it, which is why we do not sell in some of them.

Every formula goes through a 28-day challenge test for microbial stability, a 12-week accelerated stability run at 40°C, and a dermatologist-supervised patch panel of at least 30 volunteers with a minimum of a third reporting sensitive skin.

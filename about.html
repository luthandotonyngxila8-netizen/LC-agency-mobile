Home — Our story

Take care of your skin

It is on our logo because it is the whole point. LASS exists to make skincare that South Africans can actually use, on the concerns we actually have.

Since 2020

Born out of a personal battle with acne and dark spots.

LASS started the way a lot of good skincare does — with someone who had run out of options. Years of breakouts, and then the marks the breakouts left behind, which lingered long after the spots had gone. Nothing on the shelf was made with that skin in mind, or at a price that made sense to keep buying.

The turning point was turmeric: a kitchen staple with a long history of use for brightening and calming skin. Blended properly — with honey to soften it, with lemon and vitamin C to build on it — it became the first LASS product, and then a full range.

Today LASS is a registered South African business, based in Randburg, Gauteng, selling directly and through a network of independent consultants around the country.

The vision

To become the most trusted natural skincare brand in Africa.

Trust is earned in two places: in the results people see on their own skin, and in how straight we are with them about what a product can and cannot do.

What we stand for

Four things we hold to

01

Natural ingredients, used honestly

Turmeric, honey, lemon, vitamin C and pomegranate are on the label because they are in the jar. We describe what they help with — brightening, calming, conditioning — and we do not dress cosmetics up as medicine.

02

Made for the skin we serve

Acne, hyperpigmentation, dark marks and scarring are the four concerns the range is built around, because they are what our customers write to us about.

03

Affordable enough to finish the bottle

Skincare only works with consistency. Our pricing starts at R40 a product so a full routine stays within reach month after month.

04

A brand you can build a business on

Hundreds of independent consultants sell LASS in their own communities. The programme is open to anyone willing to start with a business kit and do the work.

Yours, Before

One conversation, one hide, one set of hands. Six to ten weeks, depending on what you ask for.

The Process

Five Steps. No Surprises.

  1. 01

    The conversation

    WhatsApp or come in. Tell us what you carry, where you carry it, and what failed on the last one. That is usually enough to draw from.

  2. 02

    The hide

    We show you what is on the rack, not a swatch book. For Nguni and zebra you approve the actual animal by photograph before anything is cut.

  3. 03

    The deposit

    Half up front, which buys the hide. The balance on collection. We will give you a written figure before you pay anything, and it does not move unless you change the brief.

  4. 04

    The build

    Cut, skived, stitched, burnished. Six to eight weeks for most pieces, eight to ten for crocodile. You get photographs at the cut and at the stitch.

  5. 05

    Collection

    Collect it from the workshop, or we courier it anywhere in South Africa at no charge. Your initials are stamped into the brass before it goes.

What We Make

If It Holds Something,
We Have Built It.

Weekenders, holdalls, attachés, satchels, laptop cases, belts, wallets, gun cases, cartridge bags, camera straps, passport folders and one cello case. Furniture panels and car interiors on request, in Nguni.

What we do not do: printed logos, bonded leather, anything with a plastic core, and rush jobs that would mean skipping the burnish.

Repairs & Care

We Made It. We Can Open It.

  • Stitching Free for the life of the piece. A saddle stitch fails one loop at a time, so bring it early.
  • Handles & hardware You pay materials only. Brass is replaced with brass, never plated substitutes.
  • Lining & base Quoted before we start. A relined bag is usually cheaper than any new bag worth owning.
  • Conditioning Bring it in once a year and we will do it while you wait, at no charge.

Second-hand pieces count. If it carries the AT shank, it comes back to us regardless of who owned it first.

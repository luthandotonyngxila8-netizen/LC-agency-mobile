Use

Where should I start with turmeric?

Begin with the face wash and the day cream for two weeks, then add the scrub two or three times a week. Turmeric can leave a faint tint on very fair skin — rinse well, and always patch test a new product on your inner arm first.

Care

Is it safe for sensitive or acne-prone skin?

The range is formulated with acne-prone skin in mind, but every face is different. Patch test, introduce one product at a time, and stop if something stings or stays red. If you are pregnant, on prescription acne treatment or under a dermatologist's care, check with them first.

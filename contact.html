Ship

Shipping & returns

Complimentary over $95, otherwise a flat $8. Orders leave the studio within 48 hours. If a formula does not agree with your skin, send it back within 60 days — used, half-used, it does not matter — and we refund it in full.

Refill

How the refill programme works

Buy a product once, then order refill pods at 30% less, forever. Pods are aluminium, decant in one pour, and ship in a padded paper mailer. No subscription, no lock-in — order when you actually run out.

Skin

Where should I start?

With the cleanser and the moisturiser, for two weeks. Actives are easier to judge once the barrier is calm, and most of what people diagnose as a skin problem turns out to be a cleansing problem.

Batch

Reading your batch code

The four digits on the base are the fill week and year — 1826 is week 18 of 2026. Everything is best used within 12 months of opening, and we will replace anything that arrives past its window.
